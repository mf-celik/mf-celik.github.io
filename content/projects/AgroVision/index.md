A groundbreaking ESA-funded project led by ITC Faculty in collaboration with the Forschungszentrum Jülich is revolutionizing the way we assess crop conditions with AI, integrating satellite imagery with field photos to generate comprehensive agricultural reference data.

As extreme weather events and climate change place increasing pressure on global food production, timely and accurate information on crop status is more critical than ever.

To address this, AgroVision, a project funded by the European Space Agency (ESA), aims to deploy innovative Geo-Foundation Models to provide rich, real-time insights into agricultural conditions.
