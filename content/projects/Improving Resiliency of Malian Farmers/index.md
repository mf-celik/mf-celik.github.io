The **IMPRESSYIELD** project leverages satellite data and artificial intelligence to help Malian cotton farmers adapt to climate change risks through improved yield estimation.

Cotton represents a critical economic resource in Mali, contributing 15% of GDP and 11% of total exports, while supporting approximately 4 million people. However, the crop faces significant vulnerability from drought, extreme temperatures, and rainfall variability linked to climate change.

IMPRESSYIELD creates a dynamic yield estimation map using:

- Freely available satellite Earth observation data
- Advanced AI techniques requiring minimal field data
- Deep neural network algorithms processing multi-source time-series data
- Domain adaptation methods effective in data-scarce regions

The approach offers wide coverage, repeatability, and accessibility without requiring physical field access — particularly valuable in regions with limited ground-based measurement infrastructure. The tool enables continuous crop monitoring to support farmers' climate adaptation decisions and potentially facilitate insurance products protecting against climate variability.

The project is a collaboration between Istanbul Technical University, Middle East Technical University, and agritech companies Agcurate and OKO Finance.
