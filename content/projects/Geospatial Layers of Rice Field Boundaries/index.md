This project focuses on developing a spatially explicit agricultural database for Cambodia and Vietnam, targeting rice paddy fields that represent substantial portions of regional agricultural land.

The project aims to create a geospatial database supporting three key goals:

- **Agricultural Planning** — Enabling effective farming incentive schemes
- **Financial Programs** — Supporting smart agriculture and microfinance initiatives
- **Sustainability Reporting** — Improving data on UN Sustainable Development Goal 2.4.1 regarding sustainable agriculture practices

The initiative has produced a prototype for automated crop boundary delineation with a focus on rice paddy fields, accompanied by technical documentation and training materials.

**Geographic Focus:** Vietnam and Cambodia

**Implementing Organization:** FAO Statistics Division

**Funding:** Japan's Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries
